Four rockstars, one dream.

As the stars of the heaven.

The band's name comes from a line in the Bible.
It talks about a group so big you cannot count it,
like stars in the sky or sand on the beach.
It's a big name for four guys just starting out in Dublin.
That gap between the big name and the small start is part of the story.


How it started

The band started in March 1983.
At first it was only two people,
a guitar player and a bass player,
trying out songs together.
By September that year, they found a singer
who also played guitar, and a drummer.
Now there were four, and they were a real band.

Both guitar players wrote songs, but only one of them sang.
Two writers and one singer.
This is what made the band's sound special from the start.


Cutting their teeth

Dublin's music scene in the early 1980s was small.
There were only a few clubs,
and the same bands kept playing together
and sharing the same practice rooms.
The band played some of their
first shows next to other bands who
later also became well known.
It shows just how small and simple that
scene was before anyone was famous.


The first record

Their first song came from a radio session that people loved right away.
The band paid for the recording themselves and only made a thousand copies through a small label.
At first it did not sell many copies.
But it got played on the radio and got good reviews.
That slow attention, not a big launch, is what got them noticed and helped them sign with a bigger label after.
